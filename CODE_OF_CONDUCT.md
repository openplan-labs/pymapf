# Code of Conduct

PyMAPF adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. The full text at that link applies to every project
space: issues, pull requests, discussions, and any event or channel where
someone represents the project.

## In short

We want PyMAPF to be a place where a first-year student and a senior
researcher can both ask questions without feeling out of place.

Expected:

- Being welcoming and patient, especially with people who are new to the field.
- Giving and receiving technical criticism about the work, not the person.
- Crediting other people's ideas and code.
- Owning mistakes and learning from them.

Not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or in private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers at
**erwinlejeune.pro@gmail.com**. Reports are handled confidentially. The
maintainers will act following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor
Covenant: from a private warning up to a permanent ban, depending on the
severity and on whether the behaviour continues.
